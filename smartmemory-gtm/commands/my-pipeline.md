Use the smartmemory-gtm skill and the SmartMemory CRM tools. Optional lane: $ARGUMENTS

1. Determine the user's lane (from the argument, or ask once). Query people with that `lane`.
2. Mirror the CRM views Outreach: Queue, Sent, Reddit, Skipped, Parked, Follow-ups due, Pipeline. Report counts per `outreachStatus` in a small table.
3. List: Follow-ups due (`nextStepDue` today or earlier, not REPLIED/SKIPPED), DRAFT_READY items (flag Reddit and forum ones as waiting on the team lead to post), and the QUEUE items that still need a draft.
4. For each listed person show name, channel, status, `nextStep`, `nextStepDue`. Never show contact data.
5. End with the one or two most useful next actions.
6. Read only. Change nothing.
